### What YouTube really evaluates in editing
- когда: how the algorithm rates editing · does editing affect impressions · key moments for retention
- вещь: recommendations · retention · YouTube Studio
1. Recommendations follow viewer behaviour: thumbnail clicks, retention, watch time, likes/dislikes, "not interested", satisfaction surveys. There is no separate "editing quality" score — editing works through retention.
2. Editing review — Studio → Analytics → Key moments for audience retention: intro (first 30 s), spikes, dips, continuous segments.
3. "Inauthentic" (formerly repetitive) and "reused" content policies are about monetization (YPP), not reach; reviewed by a person at channel level.
- источник: YouTube help, 2026-10-23

### Myths about editing and the algorithm
- когда: AI rates the editing · change shot every 3 seconds · voice -6 dB music -25 dB
- вещь: myths · algorithm · levels
1. "AI scores the editing", "voice-to-noise ratio affects reach", "speech-picture semantics improves targeting" — YouTube never said so (speech is recognised for captions and chapters).
2. "Change the shot every 3–7 s" is a retention-editing technique, not a requirement; screencast tutorials work at 5–10 s.
3. "Voice −6…−12 dB, music −25…−32 dB" are fader peaks, not loudness — measure in LUFS.
- источник: review of circulated advice, 2026-10-23

### How recommendations and view counting work
- когда: recommendation signals · satisfaction surveys · engaged views · view counted from first frame
- вещь: recommendations · engaged views · satisfaction
1. Signals: watch and search history, subscriptions, likes/dislikes, "not interested", "don't recommend channel", satisfaction surveys. Do not hold viewers by deceit (dragged loops, clickbait without payoff) — dissatisfied viewers lower reach.
2. Since 2026-08-24 public views count from the first frame; retention, CTR, AVD and recommendations use "engaged views".
3. A muted home-feed preview also counts as a view — the first 3 s without sound matter even more.
- источник: YouTube help and blog, 2026-10-23

### What is not monetized on YouTube
- когда: video monetization rules · inauthentic content · AI voice and monetization · repetitive content
- вещь: YPP · monetization · AI
1. Not monetized: templated/repetitive content (slideshows or scrolling text without value, one story template across many videos); compilations of unrelated clips for shock; AI personas giving advice on sensitive topics, including finance.
2. Allowed: AI in editing, sound and light; identical intros/outros; series with a repeating format.
3. Reports from 2026: screencasts with AI voice demonetized; a stock-footage channel lost monetization for months; false positives happen with re-uploads.
4. A real voice and face of the author is the safest basis for advice content.
- источник: YouTube clarification 2026-07-13 and creator reports

### Test titles and thumbnails with Test & Compare
- когда: Test & Compare · A/B test thumbnail · test video title · compare thumbnails
- вещь: Test & Compare · thumbnail · title
1. Studio, desktop only: up to 3 variants of title and/or thumbnail.
2. The winner is by share of watch time, not CTR; a test runs up to 2 weeks.
3. Test 3 really different ideas, not colour variants.
- ловушка: editing the title or thumbnail during a test resets it
- источник: YouTube help, 2026-10-23

### A tutorial that an AI summary cannot retell
- когда: how-to video loses views · tutorial losing traffic · AI summaries in search
- вещь: tutorial · screencast · deep zoom
1. How-to niches lose clicks to AI search summaries: give what text cannot — the process on a real interface, personal experience, files and templates; chapters are a must.
2. Clean deep zooms: instead of a screen recording take a very large screenshot (DevTools device with high DPR, 4K+) and move a smooth camera over it in the editor.
- источник: creator reports, 2026-10-23

### Package a video: title, thumbnail, first 30 seconds
- когда: video packaging · title length · how many words on a thumbnail
- вещь: title · thumbnail · packaging
1. Title ~50 characters in plain words; a thumbnail reads in 1–2 s, 2–4 words of text.
2. An idea that cannot be clearly packaged into title + thumbnail is not filmed.
3. The first 30 s of the edit continue exactly this packaging.
- источник: Paddy Galloway, 2026-10-23
