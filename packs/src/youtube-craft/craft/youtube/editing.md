### Edit the first 5 seconds
- когда: video hook · first seconds of the video · start of the video
- когда: viewers leave in the first seconds · confirm the click
- вещь: hook · thumbnail · title
1. The first 30 s continue the thumbnail and the title, they are not an "intro"; aim for ≥ 70 % retention at 0:30 (compare with "typical" in Studio).
2. 0:00–0:05: the first sentence repeats or strengthens the title's promise; voice from frame 2–3, cut the silence at the start.
3. The first frame is a visual anchor from the thumbnail (same screen, number, result) with a slow push-in 100→110 %.
4. First 10 s formula: what the video is about and why → the common opinion → the turn against it.
- ловушка: no stock whoosh/riser and harsh zoom in the first 2–3 s — viewers recognise a "template hook" and leave
- источник: creators' practice, 2026-10-23

### Edit the hook 0:05–0:30: stakes, loop, bridge
- когда: hook 5 to 30 seconds · open loop in video · bridge into the main part
- вещь: hook · J-cut · open loop
1. 0:05–0:15 stakes: why this is not a 10-second search — a common mistake and its cost, "people usually advise X, but…". Shot change by 5–6 s, 2–3 big words or a number on screen.
2. 0:15–0:25 loop: the steps of the video and one non-obvious point saved for later; 2–3 quick teasers, the result blurred or covered.
3. 0:25–0:30 bridge: "so…" + a J-cut (the next voice starts over the old picture).
- ловушка: no "well, let's begin", "step one", subscribe request, intro card or logo in the hook
- источник: creators' practice, 2026-10-23

### Check the hook before export
- когда: check the hook · muted preview test · pauses in the hook
- вещь: hook · muted preview · pauses
1. Muted test 0:00–0:04: the home feed plays previews without sound — motion, a change, big graphics, not a talking head.
2. 0:25–0:35: no music intro, no subscribe call, no breath before "let's start".
3. Pauses in the hook ≤ 0.2 s (4–6 frames at 30 fps); breaths at the start cut tight.
- источник: creators' practice, 2026-10-23

### Place pauses in the voice
- когда: pauses between sentences · length of pauses · pauses sound robotic
- когда: trim pauses in voiceover · identical pauses
- вещь: pauses · voice · rhythm
1. Inside a sentence — leave as is; between sentences 0.25–0.45 s; after a finished thought 0.6–0.9 s; between topics 0.9–1.4 s; in the hook ≤ 0.2 s.
2. Neighbouring pauses differ by ±0.05–0.15 s; length comes from meaning (after a question, before a number, after a conclusion — longer), not a timer.
3. The text decides the pause, not the raw recording: each may become longer or shorter.
4. No silence > 1.2 s in the mix: long voice pauses are filled with background or music.
- ловушка: do not "trim the finished voiceover to a norm" — pauses become uniform and robotic
- источник: owner's rule, 2026-10-23

### Cut the voice and place cuts
- когда: remove takes and breaths · where to cut · jump cut · J/L cut
- вещь: cuts · jump cut · J/L cut
1. Cut breaths, "uh", hesitations and duplicates — keep the last take.
2. Cut in breathing pauses, not mid-thought; leave 3–6 frames of "air" after the last word.
3. Punch-in on a jump cut keeps the eyes at the same point; hide 3–4+ jump cuts in a row under screencast or B-roll.
4. Face ↔ screencast ↔ graphics joins are J/L cuts (sound leads or lags 5–10 frames), never mid-word.
5. Keep live moments (a short laugh, a pause to think) — they build trust next to AI content.
6. A digression from the title's topic is a cut candidate: retention dips are often there.
- источник: creators' practice, 2026-10-23

### How often to change the picture
- когда: how often to change the shot · pace of shot changes · static frame too long
- вещь: pace · shot change · Ken Burns
1. A visual change at least every 10 s (target 5–8 s): screencast zoom 100→108–115 %, a shot change, graphics, an accent caption, cursor highlight.
2. Lengths vary: short 1.5–2 s and long 4–6 s with a slow zoom. Hook — every 1.5–3 s, screencast — every 5–10 s.
3. A frame with no motion ≤ 4 s; long holds get a slow drift (Ken Burns 100→105 %).
4. The picture follows the sentence, not a timer: show what is being said.
- ловушка: not hyper-editing — mechanical zooms every 0.5–2 s, constant graphics and repeated SFX hurt adult audiences in 2026
- источник: creators' practice, 2026-10-23

### The video must not look machine-assembled
- когда: video looks templated · identical repeats in editing · editing variety
- вещь: variety · pauses · inserts · animations
1. No technique repeats identically in a row: at most 3 pauses in a row of equal length (±30 ms), at most 4 shot changes at an equal interval (±0.1 s).
2. Neighbouring inserts use different layouts (full frame, floating card, split screen, side panel); one layout ≤ ~30 % of a block.
3. Text animation and transition not twice in a row; vary direction, position, duration ±10–20 %.
4. One SFX not twice in a row — 2–3 variants per event, level ±2 dB; zooms differ in direction, speed and end scale (104–112 %).
5. Structure and linking phrases are not copied between videos.
- проверка: watch 2–3 minutes from the middle asking "can I guess the template?"
- источник: owner's rule, 2026-10-23

### Edit the middle and the end of the video
- когда: middle of the video · re-hook · retention after 30 seconds · video ending · end screen · chapters
- вещь: re-hook · end screen · chapters
1. Every ~2 min a re-hook candidate: a question, a teaser of the next block, a shot change — only earned ones.
2. A block ends with a pointer to the next reason to watch, not "so, that's done".
3. Rhythm "calm — burst": a change every 5–10 s; every 2–3 min a burst of 4–8 quick changes.
4. ≈2 s without sound and motion reads as "the video ended".
5. Ending ≤ 20 s: the result in 1–2 sentences, end screen 5–10 s leading to one video.
6. Chapters: the first at 0:00, ≥ 3 chapters of ≥ 10 s.
- источник: creators' practice, 2026-10-23
